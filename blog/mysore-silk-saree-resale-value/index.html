Mysore Silk Saree Resale Value: What Your Old Mysore Silk Is Worth

By Nakshatra Kanchipuram Society | 6 min read

For many Karnataka families, a Mysore silk saree is a matter of pride. It is light, smooth and elegant, and it is often the saree chosen for pujas, office functions and family events. If you have a few old ones and are curious about their Mysore silk saree resale value, this guide explains what drives the price, how to check if yours is genuine and how to sell it in Bangalore.

What Makes Mysore Silk Different

Mysore silk sarees are known for their pure mulberry silk, soft sheen and fine zari borders. Genuine Mysore silk is closely linked with the Karnataka Silk Industries Corporation (KSIC), and Mysore silk has a Geographical Indication (GI) tag. According to Wikipedia's page on Mysore silk, KSIC zari is described as containing about 65% silver and 0.65% gold.

Compared with a Kanchipuram, a Mysore silk saree is usually lighter, with a narrower zari border and a plainer body.

Because Mysore silk usually has less zari than a heavy Kanchipuram, its resale value is often lower per saree, but a collection of old Mysore silks can still add up to a meaningful amount. For how zari is priced, see our guide on old silk saree zari price per gram.

How to Check if Your Mysore Silk Is Genuine

  • Look for KSIC identification. Genuine KSIC sarees carry identification such as a hologram or a unique code.
  • Check the feel. Pure Mysore silk is smooth and soft, with a natural, gentle sheen.
  • Check the zari. Old pure zari darkens evenly with age. See how to check pure zari in silk saree for home tests.
  • Find the bill. An old KSIC or showroom bill helps support the evaluation.

Mysore Silk vs Other Pattu Sarees

In most Bangalore homes, Mysore silks sit alongside Kanchipuram, Dharmavaram and Banarasi sarees. Each is valued on its silk and zari, so it makes sense to evaluate them all together. Our guide to old pattu saree buyers in Bangalore covers the full selling process.

Tips to Get a Better Price for Old Mysore Silk

  1. Sell the saree whole. Do not cut the border or pallu.
  2. Keep the blouse piece. It is part of the saree's silk and zari.
  3. Bring out every Mysore silk you own. Older ones may have better zari than newer ones.
  4. Air them before the visit. Let stored sarees air in shade for a few hours.
  5. Ask for an itemised quote. Know the price of each saree before you decide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do old Mysore silk sarees have resale value?

Yes. Genuine Mysore silk sarees are valued on their pure silk and their zari. Sarees with pure zari carry silver value.

Is a Mysore silk saree worth more than a Kanchipuram?

Usually not, because Mysore silk sarees tend to be lighter with less zari. The actual value depends on the zari weight and type.

Can I sell a Mysore silk saree without the KSIC label?

Yes. A label helps, but an experienced buyer can evaluate the silk and zari without it.

Do you buy faded or damaged Mysore silk sarees?

Yes. Damaged sarees still carry zari value and are evaluated like any other saree.
